## Project Knowledge

A project knowledge bundle is available at `wiki-llm/` and follows Google Open
Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2.

Before handling architecture, deployment, business rules, historical decisions,
or operational procedures, read `wiki-llm/index.md`, then load only the linked
pages relevant to the task. Treat original project files and source packets as
authoritative when a page is review-due, expired, contested, or weakly sourced.
